How I build

Start with behavior. Stay close to the humans.

A technically correct system that nobody uses is still a failed system.

  1. 01

    Start with the behavior

    Understand who will use it and what decision they are trying to make.

  2. 02

    Translate the business problem

    Turn questions, frustrations, spreadsheets, and conflicting definitions into something buildable.

  3. 03

    Model the messy parts

    Move business logic upstream into governed, reusable models.

  4. 04

    Ship something useful

    Put a focused product in front of users. Shipping creates feedback.

  5. 05

    Stay close to the humans

    Watch what people do next—and notice the spreadsheet they still keep on the side.

Human + machine

Technology is the multiplier. People are the point.

I use AI constantly. It helps me research unfamiliar systems, write and debug code, prototype interfaces, pressure-test ideas, and move faster than I could on my own.

But the bottleneck is rarely access to more generated code. It is understanding what people need, earning their trust, explaining tradeoffs, and taking responsibility for whether the finished product is useful.

AI can supersize the work. It cannot care whether the work makes sense to the humans using it.
